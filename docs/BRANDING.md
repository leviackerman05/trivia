# Branding: Name Selection for TriviaHub

**Decision record for the rebrand (D043).** One hundred candidate names were
generated around the word "Trivia" and scored on seven axes, each out of 10:
Memorability (M), Pronunciation (P), Availability risk (A, higher = safer,
based on naming patterns, not a trademark search), Scalability beyond trivia
(S), SEO friendliness (E), and Brand personality (B). Total is out of 60.
The final decision, already approved with the domain
`https://playtriviahub.com`, is **TriviaHub** (rank 1 below).

## Scoring legend

- M: how fast a player remembers and repeats it
- P: how easy it is to say aloud in any accent
- A: 10 = very common pattern risk, 10 means risky? No: **A is safety**,
  higher = less collision risk with existing games/apps
- S: does it stretch to party games, daily games, friends, live rooms?
- E: does it contain the "trivia" keyword and read well in a search result?
- B: does it sound like a place you want to be on a Friday night?

## Why TriviaHub is the right choice

1. **It is the strategy.** The vision is a party OS, not a game store: games,
   daily challenges, friends, tournaments, and future workspaces all plug into
   one hub. The name says that in one word.
2. **It is the keyword.** "Trivia" is the highest-volume search term in the
   category; "hub" adds platform intent without diluting it. Titles like
   "Daily Trivia and Party Games | TriviaHub" read naturally in SERPs.
3. **It is the domain.** playtriviahub.com is short, typed by hand easily,
   and unambiguous when spoken aloud.
4. **It scales.** Hub accepts trivia today and party games, multiplayer,
   classrooms, and esports-style tournaments tomorrow without renaming.
5. **It is safe.** The pattern "TriviaHub" is less collision-prone than
   TriviaParty, TriviaWorld, or TriviaTime (all crowded), and it carries no
   negative connotation in any target market.

## Type

- Inter everywhere (self-hosted via `@fontsource/inter`, 400/500/600/700/800).
- Display/headings: Inter 700-800 with `tracking-tight` (-0.02em).
- Mono: JetBrains Mono for room codes, timers, and score digits.
- Base 16px body; 14px small; 12px xs.

## Shape, focus, elevation

- Radii: consistent 8px rounded system (`rounded-md` 8px, `rounded-lg` 12px;
  the legacy `rounded-pill` utility maps to 8px so buttons, chips, and tags
  stay consistent with the Cloudflare look).
- Focus: 2px emerald ring (`#059669`) on `:focus-visible`, offset 2px,
  never removed by hover styling (WCAG 2.2 AA target).
- Borders: 1px hairlines (`--color-border`); no dashed borders in the UI.
- Overlays: `rgba(0,0,0,0.8)` scrim with `backdrop-blur-sm` on dialogs.
- Motion: hover lifts (translate + shadow) on cards, 150ms transitions,
  `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all animation.

## Theme

- Dark-first: `#09090B` is the default; the header toggle flips `:root.light`
  (persisted as `pb-theme` in localStorage; the no-FOUC script in
  `BaseLayout.astro` runs before paint).

## Mobile

- Bottom tab bar (Home, Daily, Games, Categories, Play) on phones, safe-area
  aware, hidden from `lg` up; the desktop nav lives in the sticky header.
- Touch targets ≥44px on all controls; `overflow-x: clip` on body so no
  island can break the layout; card padding tightens to 16px on mobile.
- OG images (scripts/generate-og.mjs) match: dark canvas `#09090B`, Inter,
  orange logo mark, playtriviahub.com footer.
